# The Big Boy Rules

## Product
Private app for one group of friends in Madrid. It is only for club members; there is no public audience, marketing surface or sales goal.

## Users and daily use
Members open it on their phones, mostly in short visits. The two main reasons to come back each day are opening packs and looking after their card collection, and Hoy en el club: daily games, La vida del Chota and group activities. Chats, plans, missions and games support those habits.

## What makes it different
Everything comes from the group itself: members appear on the cards, El Chota lives in the club's sede, and the games use the club's own messages and plans.

## Brand commitments
- Keep the name The Big Boy Rules and the BB seal.
- Keep El Chota and the sede at the centre of the app.
- Keep card art, frames and rarity colours as they are.
- Selected and pressed interface states stay neutral, never yellow or gold. The project rules in AGENTS.md apply.
- Language: informal Spanish from Spain.
- Visual standard: a polished, conventional dark mobile app, with no themed concept. Its quality references are Instagram, Apple Games and Wallet, and Discord.

## Platform
web

The same web interface ships on iOS and Android through Capacitor, so it must feel installed on a phone. New cards reach members through a remote catalogue without an app update.

## Accessibility
Text stays readable at phone size, keyboard focus stays visible and neutral, and reduced motion is respected.
