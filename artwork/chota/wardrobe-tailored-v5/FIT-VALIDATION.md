# Acceptance for the dressed character

Use real composed characters at their shipped scale and enlarged with nearest
neighbour sampling. A garment being nonempty or covering its old mask does not
prove fit; the user rejected those earlier checks.

Inspect all67 poses, particularly waist/crotch and both sleeve/cuff interfaces.
Review every material family, then animation cycles in the room and daily dilemma.

Useful geometric/raster checks for the new shared rig:

- Neck, shoulder/sleeve and wrist contacts have no transparent gap. The head and
  paws remain the established character's artwork; no rectangular old-shirt
  fragment is restored under the jaw.
- Trouser waistband overlaps the shirt hem; both articulated legs join the
  pelvis. Each continuous cuff overlaps its corresponding shoe upper at the
  same ankle. No shoe or hand is an isolated opaque component.
- Clothing uses the original pose topology and material shading, with no
  independent neutral-body warp moving the cuffs, legs or shoes. Decomposed
  material and anatomy layers must reconstruct the same coherent actor. Any
  supplemental mesh shares joint vertices and intentionally overlaps at seams.
- The selected anatomical parts are removed before clothes are composed;
  hidden body/old-clothing contours cannot protrude around a narrower garment.
  Bare parts remain fur, with independently removed slots respected.
- Speech changes the face without replacing the dressed torso. Interrupting
  speech/walk/gesture preserves a matching sheet/index for the complete actor.
- Fitting and checkout use the same composition without changing the actual
  equipped outfit; room/viewer/dilemma/games/cameos show the committed result.

Check a complete outfit, each bare slot, all bare, a coat over the outfit, legacy
visual defaults, account reset and late outfit loads. Source changes must retain
server inventory/ownership/prices and existing controls. Physical native runtime
checks are separate from browser/fixture checks and must not be claimed unless
actually performed.

Keep authoring references and rejected pilots out of runtime asset lists.
